7. A backward-support candidate is review-only. It does not become confirmed or probable.
8. The opposite member of the pair is never assigned the opposite generation.
9. No recursive propagation is performed. A backward-support row can never become a source.
10. Decisions, preview, baseline, seed, and database data are not modified.
